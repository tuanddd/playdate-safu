Crack the safe. With the crank.

Safu is a small safe-cracking game for Playdate. The crank is the dial: one turn of the crank is one turn of the knob, with a tick for every detent. Turn slowly, listen for the loud K-CHIK!, feel the dial shake, reverse direction and find the next one. Three sweet spots, then pull the handle.

A run lasts three minutes at most. The loop is short on purpose, and the modifiers keep every safe different.

How to play

  • Crank turns the dial. Clockwise, counterclockwise, clockwise.
  • Turn too fast and your progress resets. Turn too slow and the clock runs out.
  • D-pad Down pulls the handle. Pull it before all 3 spots are found and you start over.
  • B opens the pause menu, with a catalogue of every modifier.

New to safes? Press B on the title screen for a two-step tutorial with no timer.

13 modifiers, 3 per run

Every run deals three modifiers. Pairs that cancel each other out are never dealt together, and a few pairs make for a hard run.

  • BLACKOUT: It is too dark to see. Listen for loud clicks.
  • TOO LOUD: The clicks are silent. Watch for dial shakes.
  • SCRAMBLED: Try both directions for each sweet spot.
  • FOUR TUMBLERS: Find 4 sweet spots, then press Down.
  • WANDERING: Sweet spots move when you stop turning.
  • DECOY: Fake clicks end in a buzz, with no shake.
  • ONE SHOT: Press Down too soon and you lose.
  • GUARD: When you hear steps, stop for 3 seconds.
  • NITRO: Tilt to keep the liquid from spilling.
  • KEYPAD: Hold the dial still. Enter the arrows.
  • GEAR MESH: Wait for the gap. Press Down to catch it.
  • SPOTLIGHT: Tilt to reveal the pin. Crank to find its spot.
  • DUST JAM: Puff the dust away (or hold Up). Free the stuck pin.

Features

  • 1:1 crank-to-dial mapping with a tick on every detent
  • Crisp 1-bit dithered art at 50 fps, with manga sound-effect lettering
  • Uses the crank, the D-pad, the accelerometer and the microphone
  • Headphones recommended: some modifiers are all about sound

Install

Download Safu.pdx.zip and sideload it from play.date/account/sideload. You can also unzip it and open Safu.pdx in the Playdate Simulator.
